Diary

17.02.25 - We sat down to analyze the requirements of the game. We decided to collect game and execution ideas. We brainstormed for
           a while to find a logic that we could follow through the process of implementation, such as possible network communication.
           In the beginning, our focus was on how rather than what.

23.02.25 - Finally, we collected all our ideas for the game. We considered both round-based and real-time games. Due to our fascination
           with Monopoly and other strategic games, we decided to try our luck at implementing a better version of Monopoly.

27.02.25 - We started to create some UML diagrams for our existing classes to visualize how everything should work. We had a little
           knowledge gap between each of us, and we tried to bridge that over the last few days/weeks.

01.03.25 - After finalizing the plan, we discussed who was responsible for what. We divided the group into three parts. Pascal is
           responsible for the network protocol. He will design and visualize what the communication between the server and client
           should look like, implementing methods of messaging. Dominik is responsible for the basics of the GUI; he will build a
           basic yet sleek user interface. Domonkos is in charge of the server and client architecture. He will handle new client
           connections to the server.

04.03.25 - A few days away from our first milestone, we are preparing the final modifications regarding who is doing what. Our
           goal is to implement a robust and modular base so we can easily add our ideas in the future.
